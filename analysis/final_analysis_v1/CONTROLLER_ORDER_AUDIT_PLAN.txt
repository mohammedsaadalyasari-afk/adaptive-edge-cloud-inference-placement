FINAL CONTROLLER-ORDER AUDIT
============================

This is a read-only integrity audit.

For every one of the 30 final experimental runs:

1. Load the frozen 289-row CPU-load schedule.
2. Load the archived load_controller_events.jsonl file.
3. Require exactly:
   - 1 controller_start event
   - 7 phase_transition events
   - 289 decision_applied events
   - 1 controller_stop event
4. Verify the ordered decision_applied sequence against the
   frozen schedule.
5. decision_number and replay_seq are mandatory and must
   match exactly for all 289 decisions.
6. Where present in the event schema, source_line, phase and
   worker count are additionally checked exactly.
7. Failure to extract decision_number or replay_seq from any
   decision_applied event fails the audit.
8. No experiment or raw measurement is modified.
